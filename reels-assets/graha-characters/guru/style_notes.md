# Style notes — Guru / Brihaspati (Jupiter)

## Keep consistent
Yellow/gold teacher robes; blessing mudra; scripture/palm leaves; warm benevolent elder presence.

## Avoid
Comedic fat-guru caricature; empty hands with no scripture cue.

## Consistency checklist (series-wide)
- Same painted-cinematic mythic film key-art treatment
- Centered vertical framing with caption headroom
- Temple-cosmic atmosphere (not flat studio)
- Readable silhouette at phone Reel size
- No watermarks, no on-image text, no modern clothing

## Files in this folder
- `hero_portrait.png` — 1024×1536 full scene
- `hero_square.png` — 1080×1080 center crop
- `transparent_cutout.png` — rembg RGBA isolation
- `prompt.txt` — exact prompts used

## Remotion usage notes
- Prefer `hero_portrait.png` for Ken Burns (slow zoom/pan)
- Prefer `transparent_cutout.png` for aura/particle compositing over custom BG
- Layered puppet parts not available yet for this graha
