The math behind the Reels delivery score is based on a combination of watch time, engagement probability, and content relevance. While we don't share the exact formula, the system uses machine learning to predict how likely a viewer is to find your astrology Reel valuable based on their past behavior.
Here are the primary components that make up the "math" of your Reel's distribution:
Key Scoring Factors
Watch Time and Completion Rate: This is the most critical factor. The system calculates the percentage of the video watched. If a high number of Meta Accounts watch your spirituality Reel to the very end, it signals extreme relevance, triggering a higher score.
Engagement Probability: The algorithm predicts the likelihood of a Like, Comment, or Share. It looks at the ratio of these actions relative to total views. A Reel with 200 views and 20 likes has a much higher score than one with 1,000 views and only 5 likes.
Relevance Signals: The system processes the audio track, visual elements, and your captions (like your Samudra Manthan hashtags). It matches these against the "interest profiles" of viewers who frequently engage with Vedic Astrology content.
The "Test Group" Math
When you post, the Reel is shown to a small sample (e.g., 100-200 Meta Accounts). The "score" is calculated based on how that specific group reacts. If the engagement falls below a certain threshold, the distribution slows down, which is why you see your views plateau at 200.
